# ShareSpace AI — Hackathon Pitch

## One-line pitch
ShareSpace AI turns idle institutional resources into trusted, fairly allocated opportunities—with an auditable journey from discovery to return.

## 60-second pitch
Colleges, labs and makerspaces own expensive tools and facilities that sit unused, while students and early-stage teams cannot afford access. A normal booking calendar cannot handle safety qualifications, fair allocation, handover evidence or damage disputes. ShareSpace AI connects nearby users with resources and supports the entire lifecycle: discovery, eligibility checks, booking-conflict analysis, fair-access recommendations, owner approval, check-in, condition evidence, returns, incidents and appeals. Our specialist agents explain what they checked and why a request needs review; they do not override the owner or invent real-world verification. Owners stay in control, decisions have reasons, and important actions are auditable. The goal is not just more bookings—it is safer, fairer and more accountable use of resources that already exist.

## Suggested 3-minute live demo
1. **Student discovery (25 sec):** sign in as `student@demo.com`; search a nearby resource and open its conditions.
2. **Booking (25 sec):** request a slot with a clear purpose; show that the request is not instantly approved.
3. **Agent trace (40 sec):** open AI Agents and explain eligibility, booking checks, fair-access result, evidence and human decision boundary.
4. **Provider review (30 sec):** sign out, sign in as `provider@demo.com`, review the request and record a reasoned decision.
5. **Handover and return (35 sec):** if the workflow allows with the chosen booking status, show check-in condition, optional photo, QR reference, return condition and incident report.
6. **Accountability (25 sec):** show incident evidence, appeal/review workflow and audit history.

If the live demo data does not support a step, show the relevant page with clearly labelled demo data or skip it—do not claim an untested action worked.

## Judge differentiators
- Resource lifecycle, not just a booking calendar.
- Specialist, explainable rule-based agents integrated into the workflow.
- Fair access through transparent limits/queue rules.
- Condition evidence and incident review with human oversight.
- Appeal path and auditable decision history.

## Honest technical statement
The current build uses rule-based agent workflows and optional LLM-enhanced explanations where configured. Photo evidence is stored for human inspection; it is not automatically proof of damage. QR codes identify a booking reference and do not physically secure equipment. Qualification checks in a demo are not real-world credential verification. State only what has been tested in the live environment.
